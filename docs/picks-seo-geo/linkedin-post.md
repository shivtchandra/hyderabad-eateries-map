# LinkedIn launch post

## Post (paste as is)

I asked ChatGPT and Google where to eat biryani in Hyderabad.

They cited a blog from 2018.

So I read 56,192 r/hyderabad comments instead.

Not with AI. I counted people. One person praising a place five times counts once. Promo accounts don't count at all.

Here's what Hyderabad actually says:

→ Bawarchi, Pista House and Paradise get named most for biryani. But "most named" isn't "most loved".
→ Paradise: 198 locals still recommend it. A vocal few call it overrated. The real complaint is "it used to be better".
→ The question people ask most specifically isn't "best biryani". It's "where do I get mutton without a plate of bones".
→ Shah Ghouse vs Pista House is the most argued pair: 48 people named both in the same comment.

What I built from it:

1. A map that opens on what locals recommend, not on ads or ratings.
2. "What to order" on every place, ranked by how many people name each dish.
3. Anyone can add a dish they loved. It goes public once a second person agrees.
4. 35 question pages ("Is Paradise overrated?", "Mehfil or Shadab?") with live polls.

The surprising part: the internet's answer to "best food in Hyderabad right now" is mostly years old. Google literally shows "Missing: now?" under the results.

Fresh, honest, counted. That's the bet.

Which Hyderabad place do you think is overrated? I'll add the best answers to the poll.

## Why it's written this way (reach tactics)
- **Hook before "see more":** the first 2 lines create a gap (ChatGPT citing a 2018 blog). Short lines, one idea per line.
- **Specific numbers** (56,192 / 198 / 48) beat adjectives and stop the scroll.
- **A mildly contrarian local take** (Paradise "used to be better") pulls Hyderabad people into the comments, and comments drive reach.
- **Link in the first comment, not the post:** LinkedIn down-ranks posts with outbound links.
- **Ends on an easy, opinionated question.** It's quick to answer, and every reply gets more distribution.
- **3–5 hashtags, no emoji walls.** The arrows are plain text.

## Posting tips
- Post Tuesday–Thursday, 8:30–10 am IST. Reply to every comment in the first 60–90 minutes.
- Add a 4–6 slide carousel (PDF) if possible:
  - "ChatGPT cited a 2018 blog";
  - "56,192 comments";
  - "Paradise: used to be better?";
  - "The bones question";
  - "Shah Ghouse vs Pista House";
  - "Vote".

  Carousels get longer dwell time.
- Reshare the poll result as a follow-up post one week later ("You voted: 64% say Paradise used to be better").
